# Worship Wheel — Charl's Action List

**Date**: 9 March 2026
**Reference**: PRD - Worship Wheel Assessment Tool v1.0

This is your checklist of everything needed from you to get the Worship Wheel live. Items are grouped by when they're needed.

---

## BEFORE DEVELOPMENT BEGINS (Blocking)

These must be completed before any development work can start.

- [ ] **Review and sign off the PRD** — Read through the full PRD document and confirm you're happy with the overall concept, user journey, and scope
- [ ] **Approve the 16 assessment questions** — Review each question's wording, the 4 answer options per question, and the point values assigned to each answer. Confirm they accurately reflect skill levels for the target audience
- [ ] **Confirm the 8 elements** — Verify that Fretboard, Harmony, Melody, Rhythm, Tone, Theory, Technique, and Aural are the correct 8 elements (and that Practice is intentionally excluded)
- [ ] **Approve the scoring model** — Confirm: 2 questions per element, averaged to give a 1–10 element score, summed for an overall score out of 80
- [ ] **Approve the 5 profile archetypes** — Review The Rhythm Player, The Theory Head, The Campfire Strummer, The Balanced Beginner, and The Uneven Intermediate. Flag any you'd like to change, add, or remove
- [ ] **Approve the CTA score-band mapping** — Confirm these recommendations make sense:
  - Below 30 → Free Worship Wheel Training Video
  - 30–50 → 90-Day Challenge
  - 50–65 → WGS Academy Membership
  - Above 65 → Advanced Workshops / Masterclass
